Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026 · Applies to MeetVox Card for iPhone

The short version. MeetVox Card has no account, no sign-up and no servers of ours. Your cards and the contacts you scan are stored on your iPhone and backed up to your own iCloud, which we cannot read. There is no analytics service, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

The one exception is the optional team feature. If you choose to publish or join a team, a small amount of team information is stored in a shared area of iCloud that we can see. That is described in full below.

Who we are

MeetVox Card is an iPhone app published by Antoine Debroye. If you have a question about this policy or about your data, email antoine.debroye@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you create in the app is written to your device's own storage:

  • Your cards — name, pronouns, job title, company, email address, phone number, website, LinkedIn and other links, postal address, a short bio, and any profile photo, company logo or banner you add.
  • Contacts you capture — the details read from a QR code, a paper business card or an event badge, plus any notes, tags, "met at" note and photo attached to them.
  • Usage counters — simple tallies such as how many times you tapped Share. These are shown to you inside the app and are never transmitted anywhere. They are not analytics; nobody but you ever sees them.

Your iCloud backup

So your cards and contacts survive reinstalling the app and appear on your other devices, they are copied to the private database of your own iCloud account.

We cannot read any of this. A private iCloud database belongs to you. Apple does not give app developers access to it, and we have no way to view, export or recover its contents. If you lose access to your Apple Account, we cannot help retrieve your data.

If you are not signed in to iCloud the app still works fully. It keeps everything locally and tells you plainly in Settings that nothing is backed up.

Team features, and what we can see

Publishing or joining a team is entirely optional. Most people will never use it. If you do use it, the following is written to a shared area of iCloud rather than your private one:

When you…What is stored
Publish a team The team name, an optional company domain, the brand colour, which card templates are allowed, any team logo and banner image you upload, an anonymous identifier for your iCloud account, and the time of the update.
Join a team The team code, the display name you type when joining, an anonymous identifier for your iCloud account, the date you joined, and your role.

The identifier mentioned above is generated by Apple and is specific to this app. It is not your Apple Account, your email address or your device.

Be aware of two things. First, any signed-in MeetVox Card user who knows a team's code can read that team's details, including the display names of its members — that is how joining a team works. Choose what you type as your display name accordingly.

Second, because this is a shared area rather than your private one, we can see it through Apple's administrative console. We do not use it for anything other than running the feature and fixing faults.

Reading team data requires being signed in to iCloud. It is not open to the public internet.

What we never collect

  • No account, no username, no password. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No analytics, product telemetry or crash reporting service.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no profiling.
  • No location data.
  • No third-party software development kits of any kind. The app contains no code from analytics, advertising or social networks.
  • No payment information. The app does not sell anything.

The app makes no network connections except to Apple's iCloud. There is no server of ours for it to talk to.

Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhy, and what happens
CameraTo scan QR codes, business cards and event badges. Recognition happens on your device. Images are not uploaded.
Photos (choose)Only to pick a profile photo, logo or banner, or to read a card image you select.
Photos (add)Only to save your QR code or a video-call background when you ask for it.
ContactsTo save a scanned person to your address book, and to let you fill your own card from a contact you pick. The app reads only the contact you choose; it never browses your address book.

Every permission is optional. Decline any of them and the rest of the app continues to work.

Sharing your card

When you share a card by QR code, AirDrop, Message, email or Apple Wallet, the details travel directly from your device to the recipient. Nothing passes through us.

Apple Wallet passes are built and cryptographically signed on your iPhone. No server is involved in creating them.

Once you have handed your details to someone, what they do with them is outside our control and outside this policy.

Deleting your data

Cards and contacts. Settings → Delete all data removes every card and contact from your iPhone and from your iCloud backup. Deleting the app also removes its local storage. Export your contacts to CSV first if you want to keep them, because this cannot be undone.

Team records are the exception. The current version of the app cannot delete a team you published or a membership you created. If you want those removed, email antoine.debroye@gmail.com and we will delete them for you. We intend to make this possible in the app itself.

How long data is kept

Data on your device and in your private iCloud is kept until you delete it. Team records in the shared area are kept until you ask us to remove them, or until the container is reset.

Children

MeetVox Card is a tool for exchanging professional contact details. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or erase personal data held about you, and to complain to a data protection regulator.

In practice, almost all of your data is already in your own hands: it is on your device and in your own iCloud, and the app can export your contacts to a CSV file at any time. For team records, which are the only data we can access, email us and we will provide or delete them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and describe the change here. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
